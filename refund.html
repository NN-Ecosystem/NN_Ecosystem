Refund Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

We want Credit purchases to be fulfilled correctly. If a payment was duplicated, charged incorrectly, or Credits were not delivered after a completed payment, contact us so the transaction can be investigated.

Requesting a refund

Contact nguyenhoainho@gmail.com with the account email and provider transaction/order reference. Do not send card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials.

Consumed digital Credits

Where permitted by applicable law, Credits already consumed for completed usage may not be refundable. This does not limit mandatory consumer rights or remedies for duplicate, unauthorized, incorrect, or failed fulfillment.

Provider adjustments

Approved refunds and chargebacks may require corresponding Credit/ledger adjustments to preserve the integrity of the Core economy. Refund handling does not create a separate wallet.

Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy excludes rights that cannot legally be excluded in the customer's jurisdiction.
