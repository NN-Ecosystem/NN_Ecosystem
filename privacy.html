Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NN Ecosystem processes information when you use the NN Ecosystem website, Core Factory, connected Cloud services, Marketplace/community features, support channels, and Core Credit payment flows.

1. Privacy principles

Core Factory is designed around a local-first architecture. Where a capability can operate locally, application and runtime data may remain on the user's device. Information is transmitted to NN Ecosystem or a third-party service when a connected feature requires it, when the user requests a Cloud or remote service, when account/economy operations must be verified, or when the user intentionally submits information such as feedback or support requests.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Account information: email address, account identifiers, verification status, and related authentication/security records.
  • Core registration information: registered Core identifiers, device/Core association, entitlement, permission, usage-policy, and activation/grant state needed to operate protected services.
  • Economy information: Core Credit orders, wallet-ledger entries, credit grants, balances/projections, fulfillment status, refunds/adjustments, and transaction references.
  • Operational and security information: service requests, audit/security events, diagnostic information, error information, and records required to maintain service integrity.
  • Community and support information: name, email address, feedback/comment text, support messages, and information you voluntarily provide.
  • Website usage counters: the public website currently uses Firebase Realtime Database to maintain aggregate page-view, online-user, and selected link-click counters. A temporary random browser-session identifier is used for online presence and is removed when the connection ends.

3. Local Core data

Core Factory separates replaceable application binaries from persistent user and ecosystem data. Local Engines, Pipelines, Plugins, Nodes, jobs, settings, and other user-created or runtime data may be stored on the user's device. NN Ecosystem does not automatically receive all local Core data merely because Core Factory is installed.

If you choose a feature that connects to Cloud, Host/remote services, an external provider, or another connected capability, the data required for that feature may be transmitted as part of the requested operation.

4. Website analytics and community features

The NN Ecosystem website uses Firebase services for lightweight aggregate site counters. The website also communicates with NN Ecosystem Cloud endpoints to display and submit community feedback. Feedback submissions may include the name, email address, and comment entered by the user. Email addresses are used for feedback identity/support purposes and are not intended to be displayed publicly by default.

5. Payments and Core Credits

Core Credit checkout may be processed by Paddle or another configured payment provider. Payment providers process payment credentials under their own privacy and security practices. NN Ecosystem does not need to store raw payment-card numbers or card security codes.

NN Ecosystem may retain the Core Credit order, provider transaction/reference identifiers, payment status, fulfillment result, refund/chargeback status, and related ledger records needed for fulfillment, reconciliation, fraud prevention, customer support, financial records, and service integrity.

6. Third-party services

NN Ecosystem may rely on third-party infrastructure or service providers to deliver specific features. Current examples can include GitHub Pages for public website hosting, Firebase for website counters, Paddle for payment processing, and Cloud hosting providers for NN Ecosystem server-side services. These providers may process technical information necessary to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. How information is used

We use information as reasonably necessary to:

  • create, verify, secure, and support NN Ecosystem accounts;
  • register and authorize Core installations and connected services;
  • provide entitlements, permissions, usage policies, wallet and Core Credit functionality;
  • process and reconcile Core Credit orders and payment confirmations;
  • operate Marketplace, community, support, Host/remote, and Cloud features;
  • maintain reliability, diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and protect platform security;
  • meet applicable legal, accounting, dispute-resolution, and regulatory obligations.

8. Data retention

Retention depends on the type and purpose of the information. Long-lived account, entitlement, economy, payment-reference, ledger, fraud-prevention, and security records may be retained where needed for service integrity or legitimate legal/business requirements. Temporary operational and diagnostic data is intended to use bounded retention where practical. Community/support records may be retained while they remain useful for support, moderation, security, or service improvement.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service. Sensitive server-side credentials and payment-provider secrets are intended to remain on trusted server infrastructure rather than in public client code. No networked service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their account credentials and local device.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you. Some financial, ledger, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, or legal records may need to be retained even when other account information can be deleted.

You can choose not to submit optional community feedback or support information. You can also choose not to purchase Core Credits. Some connected features cannot operate without transmitting the information necessary to provide that feature.

11. Children

NN Ecosystem and Core Factory are not intentionally directed to children where parental consent or other special legal requirements would apply. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

12. International processing

NN Ecosystem services and service providers may process information in countries other than the user's country. Where applicable, we rely on the service provider's safeguards and applicable legal mechanisms for such processing.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Core Factory, NN Ecosystem, applicable law, or service providers evolve. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.
