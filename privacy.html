Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

NN Ecosystem processes the minimum account, Core registration, entitlement, order, payment-reference, wallet-ledger, security, operational, and support data needed to provide Core Factory services.

What we process

This may include email/account identifiers, registered Core identifiers, entitlements, Credit orders and ledger records, payment-provider transaction references, security/audit events, and information you submit for support or community feedback.

Payments

Payment details are processed by the external payment provider. NN Ecosystem does not need to store raw card details. We retain provider references and payment status needed for fulfillment, reconciliation, fraud prevention, support, and financial records.

Retention and security

Long-lived account/economy records and financial history are retained as required for service integrity and legitimate business/legal needs. Temporary operational records use bounded retention where practical. Access to server-side secrets is restricted to Cloud infrastructure.

Your choices

You may contact us about account/privacy questions and applicable access, correction, or deletion rights. Some financial, security, or ledger records may need to be retained where legally or operationally required.
